Frequently Asked Questions

How does RetailPulse calculate market traffic and demand forecasts?

RetailPulse combines multi-source web intelligence with internal point-of-sale datasets stored in SQLite. For forward-looking projections, we train a 2-layer PyTorch LSTM neural network with a 30-day sequential lookback window, benchmarking against lagged ensemble models to ensure high predictive fidelity.

Why does India's retail landscape skew overwhelmingly mobile-first?

Over 80%+ of India's retail traffic occurs on smartphones, driven by widespread 5G mobile data access, mobile payment integration (UPI), and app-first shopping ecosystems from Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, and Samsung.

How does the system prevent stockouts and optimize inventory reorder points?

The platform calculates Safety Stock (SS = Z × σ_L) at a 95% service level and sets Reorder Points (ROP = d × L + SS). When current inventory falls below ROP, real-time alert triggers recommend immediate replenishment batches.

How is customer churn prediction executed without data leakage?

We enforce strict temporal cutoff windows for feature engineering (recency, frequency, monetary spend, tenure) before the target churn window. An XGBoost champion model achieves a verified ROC-AUC of 0.9615 and 90.4% accuracy.
