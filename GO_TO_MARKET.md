# RetailPulse Enterprise: Commercial Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

**Category**: B2B AI Retail Supply Chain & Customer Intelligence Platform  
**Target Market**: Mid-Market & Enterprise Multi-Channel Brands ($5M–$100M GMV)  

---

## 1. Target Customer Profile (ICP)

- **Primary Persona**: VP of Retail Operations, Chief Commercial Officer (CCO), Head of Supply Chain & Inventory.
- **Brand Characteristics**:
  - Multi-channel operations: Shopify Plus store + Amazon Seller Central + Wholesale / Physical Outlets.
  - SKU catalog: 200 to 10,000 active SKUs.
  - Pain point: Experiencing either costly stockouts (lost revenue) or excess inventory holding costs ($200K+ tied up in stagnant inventory).

---

## 2. Problem Statement & Value Proposition

### 2.1 The Problem
Mid-market retail brands manage inventory and customer retention in disjointed spreadsheets:
- Static formulas fail to anticipate demand seasonality and lead-time variability.
- High-value customer churn is detected only after months of inactivity.
- Procurement teams spend 15+ hours weekly manually compiling purchase orders.

### 2.2 The RetailPulse Value Proposition
1. **Zero Guesswork Replenishment**: Statistical Safety Stock ($SS$) and Dynamic Reorder Points ($ROP$) reduce stockouts by 35% and holding costs by 22%.
2. **Predictive Churn Preemption**: Identifies at-risk customers with 96% ROC-AUC before they lapse and pushes automated retention flows directly to Klaviyo.
3. **1-Click Purchase Order Automation**: Generates verified procurement orders with delivery SLAs and dispatches them directly to suppliers.
